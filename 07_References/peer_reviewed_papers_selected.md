# Selected Peer-Reviewed Papers for the GLW CML EDA

Selected: 2026-07-09

Selection rule: peer-reviewed journal articles most directly supporting the EDA and planned modeling. The selected set emphasizes CML treatment and monitoring standards, BCR-ABL1 molecular trajectories, DMR/MR4.5/CMR and treatment-free remission, irregular or remote monitoring, Philadelphia chromosome/cytogenetic response, imatinib/TKI context, and joint longitudinal-survival modeling. The book entry from the broader reference library was excluded from this paper-only set.

## Core Clinical and Monitoring Papers

1. Hochhaus et al. (2020), European LeukemiaNet 2020 recommendations for treating chronic myeloid leukemia.
   - Use: primary guideline anchor for CML treatment, response milestones, and monitoring interpretation.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41375-020-0776-2
   - BibTeX key: `Hochhaus2020ELN`

2. Jabbour and Kantarjian (2025), Chronic Myeloid Leukemia: A Review.
   - Use: concise modern clinical background for diagnosis, treatment, and monitoring.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2025.0220
   - BibTeX key: `Jabbour2025JAMAReview`

3. Haddad and Kantarjian (2026), Chronic myeloid leukemia: incorporation of recent advances into current treatment algorithms.
   - Use: latest treatment-algorithm framing for the manuscript introduction/discussion.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41408-026-01527-6
   - BibTeX key: `Haddad2026Algorithms`

4. Senapati et al. (2023), Management of chronic myeloid leukemia in 2023 - common ground and common sense.
   - Use: practical management context linking guidelines with real-world clinical decisions.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41408-023-00823-9
   - BibTeX key: `Senapati2023CMLManagement`

5. Takahashi et al. (2024), JSH practical guidelines for hematological malignancies, 2023: leukemia-4. CML/MPN.
   - Use: recent guideline comparator, especially relevant for Asian and real-world cohorts.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s12185-024-03865-2
   - BibTeX key: `Takahashi2024JSHGuidelines`

## Molecular Response, DMR, and TFR Papers

6. Hughes et al. (2014), Early molecular response predicts outcomes in patients with CML treated with frontline nilotinib or imatinib.
   - Use: supports the EDA focus on early BCR-ABL1/log-MRD trajectory and later response.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1182/blood-2013-06-510396
   - BibTeX key: `Hughes2014EarlyMolecularResponse`

7. Rasekh et al. (2026), BCR-ABL1 Transcript <=10% IS as an early molecular-response predictor of TFR eligibility.
   - Use: directly connects early molecular response with treatment-free remission eligibility.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clml.2026.04.007
   - BibTeX key: `Rasekh2026TFRPredictor`

8. Ono et al. (2026), Early predictors of MR4.5 attainment and eligibility for TKI discontinuation.
   - Use: supports DMR/MR4.5 endpoint analysis and TKI-discontinuation motivation.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1182/bloodadvances.2026019701
   - BibTeX key: `Ono2026MR45`

9. Takahashi et al. (2026), Five-year interim analysis of J-SKI: an observational study of TKI discontinuation in Japan.
   - Use: recent long-term discontinuation evidence for TFR discussion.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s12185-026-04184-4
   - BibTeX key: `Takahashi2026JSKI`

10. Parihar et al. (2026), Optimizing Treatment-Free Remission in CML: real-world data from Eastern India.
    - Use: real-world monitoring and TFR feasibility context.
    - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s12288-025-02199-1
    - BibTeX key: `Parihar2026TFR`

11. Atallah and Sweet (2021), Treatment-Free Remission: the New Goal in CML Therapy.
    - Use: readable TFR review for framing DMR as a clinically meaningful state.
    - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11899-021-00653-1
    - BibTeX key: `Atallah2021TFR`

## Joint Longitudinal-Survival Modeling Papers

    - DOI: https://doi.org/10.18637/jss.v072.i07
    - BibTeX key: `Rizopoulos2016JMbayes`

22. Lovblom et al. (2026), A joint model for a longitudinal outcome and a progressive multistate model under a mixed observation scheme.

    - BibTeX key: `Lovblom2026MixedObservationJointModel`

23. Niu and Zhao (2026), Quantile inference for multivariate response regression in joint modeling of longitudinal and survival data.

    - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/09622802261420698
    - BibTeX key: `Niu2026QuantileJointModel`
